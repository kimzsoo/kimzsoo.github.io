Hi, I'm a student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at KAIST, where I am advised by Prof. Woojun Kim.

My long-term goal is to build robots that can understand language and navigate the real world alongside people. I explore how vision and language can guide robots toward the goals people describe, whether alone or as a team.

About

I am currently a research intern at the AI and Robotic Systems Lab at KAIST, completing my B.S. with an AI Special Designated Major.

Previously, I was a research intern at the Data Intelligence System Lab, where I contributed to work on detecting AI involvement in scientific writing through citation behavior. Alongside that, I ran my own projects on vision-language-action models and robot manipulation, which is what drew me toward robotics.

Research Interests

My research mainly focuses on vision-language navigation (VLN), with broader interests in vision-language-action (VLA), robot manipulation, and multi-agent systems.
